# Buyer-side comparison: Stage 2 Year A English Unit 1 (Narrative, Fantastic Mr Fox)

Reviewed 20 Oct 2026, from the point of view of an NSW Stage 2 teacher choosing what to buy on TpT.

## What I looked at

**Our pack** (`dist/s2-en-a-u1/`):
- the Component B deck: all 407 slides, checked on contact sheets, with 12 slides viewed at full size
- the Component A deck: 189 slides, converted to PDF and sampled
- the colour worksheets: all 44 pages
- the posters (14 pages)
- the first 16 of the 51 unit of work pages
- the 5 listing covers and the 6-page preview PDF
- the speaker notes, hyperlinks and font sizes in both PPTX files

**Competitors.** I had their public TpT previews only, so I judged what a buyer can actually see:
- **BHB**, The Busy Honey Bee, "Teaching Slides Unit 1": 5 preview pages, about 20 slide thumbnails
- **LG**, Literacy Guru 101, "Supporting slideshow, Week One": 7 preview pages, about 14 slides
- **OTH**, another seller's complete Component B unit: 11 pages covering slides, the program table, worksheets and LI/SC journal pages

The scores for competitors are partly inferred from small samples. Ours are based on the whole product.

## 2. Top 10 improvements, ranked by impact on sales

**1. Make the cover and title searchable and visibly about Fox.**
- **Problem:** Cover 1 says "NARRATIVE AND CHARACTER" and does not mention "Fantastic Mr Fox" or "Unit 1". Buyers search "Stage 2 Year A Unit 1" and "Fantastic Mr Fox", and both competitors put these words big on the thumbnail.
- **Fix the cover:** Retitle it "STAGE 2 YEAR A · UNIT 1 NARRATIVE" with the subline "for use with Fantastic Mr Fox (book not included)".
- **Fix the image:** Replace the worksheet collage with a fox photo (the Pexels fox from slide 66, or the L5 fox at night) next to two hero slides.
- **Fix the footer:** The strip "20 lessons + 20 skills · 7 worksheets × 3 levels" wraps onto two cramped lines. Make it one line, such as "Components A + B · 407 slides · 3 levels".

**2. Rebuild the preview PDF and covers 3 and 4 around our best slides, not our plainest.**
- **Problem:**
  - Preview p3 ("Inside every lesson") and cover 4 ("Teaching slides") show the word list (slides 4 and 5), an LI/SC slide and the empty slow-reveal frame. These are the least impressive slides we have.
  - Preview p3 text is cut off mid-sentence ("Writing lessons add model writing in").
  - Preview p2 is a wall of bullets.
- **Fix:** Show one full lesson as a filmstrip, using L13 slides 242 to 262. Then add a "best of" grid:
  - the I do model writing (56, 253)
  - the drag sort (54)
  - the Venn diagram (59 or 210)
  - the Freytag arc (247)
  - the hinge question with its reveal (255 and 256)
  - Choose your challenge (260)
  - the trait wall photo slide (80)
- Fix the truncated text and cut p2 to 6 bullets.

**3. Fix how the book is handled on screen.**
This is where buyers will compare us most directly with OTH and LG.
- **Problem:** The 23 slides with an empty dashed "Paste or type the passage from your copy" frame look unfinished when projected: slides 12 to 15, 34, 35, 38, 49, 72, 112, 118, 149, 177, 191, 192, 209, 227, 249, 267, 268, 296, 327 and 402. Competitors show the actual text, highlighted, and a teacher reading the preview will see our version as more work.
- **We must not reproduce the text.** Instead, make the frame look deliberate:
  - a large "Book time" panel with an open-book illustration
  - the chapter and page in 28 pt
  - "Read aloud / show under the visualiser"
  - a "What to listen for" list the class can track while the teacher reads (for example, on slide 112: "Count the small steps. Listen for short sentences.")
- **Make the editability visible:** Keep the editable text box, but make it an optional overlay rather than a big empty frame.
- **Sell it:** Say this in the listing as a feature: "copyright-safe: works with your own copy and a visualiser".

**4. Add Fox-specific, real imagery across the deck.**
- **Problem:** Apart from about 13 photos (the lesson title slides, the farm valley, the tunnel and the cellar), the pictures are generic 3D emoji that do not always fit the slide:
  - a pear on "complication" (slide 90)
  - a pumpkin on the adjective slides
  - a bone and a dog on "A good resolution" (slide 366)
  - an anvil-shaped icon on slide 366
  - a puzzle piece on "Somebody Wanted"
- **Fix:** Add licensed photos where the teaching needs a picture:
  - foxes, farm buildings, a chicken house, a cider cellar, digging machines and a flooded tunnel
  - an animal photo for Badger (slides 190 and 207)
  - a mechanical digger for L5 and L6 (slides 101 and 110)
  - a goose and a duck for Bunce (slide 35)
- **Rule:** Every "We do" slide in lessons 5 to 14 that is currently text plus emoji should get one relevant photo. BHB and OTH win on "this is obviously the Fox unit" at a glance.

**5. Break the identical lesson skeleton and trim repetitive slides.**
- **The problem:** Every lesson has the same run of slides, in the same order:
  - title
  - LI/SC
  - warm-up
  - Word
  - Yes/No
  - the MCQ, then the same MCQ again as "Check"
  - activity
  - Choose your challenge
  - Tell/Ask/Give
  - Discuss
  - Exit
- **The cost:** Repeating the same layout in the same order 20 times is the biggest reason the deck feels generated. It also pushes the count to 407, which looks heavy for a 60-minute lesson (about 19 slides each).
- **Cut the repeats:**
  - The Tell/Ask/Give slide is word-for-word identical on 7 lessons (63, 164, 239, 341, 359, 379, 397). Keep it once in L3 and once in L20, and in other lessons fold a one-line reminder into the activity slide.
  - The "Check: Which one?" reveal duplicates are about 40 slides. Make the reveal an animation (appear on click) on the same slide, rather than a second slide.
  - The "Our success criteria" checklist with the big green tick appears in most writing lessons. Merge it into the activity slide.
- **Vary the layouts:** Rotate at least four warm-up layouts instead of the same 2×2 grid every time: a quick-quiz, an image prompt, an odd-one-out and a "fix it" sentence.

**6. Fix the duplicated speaker notes.**
- **Problem:** 117 Component B slides and 23 Component A slides have the same notes paragraph printed twice. Examples: B slides 11 to 15, 17, 19, 20, 31, 33 to 36 and 38; A slides 15, 21, 28, 29 and 38. A teacher in Presenter view sees this straight away, and it reads as sloppy.
- **Fix:** De-duplicate in the engine and rebuild all 7 themes.

**7. Make the posters real posters.**
- **Problem:** Posters PDF pp2 to 14 are A4 pages of small text cards: a word bank plus the "I can" statements for each lesson. From across a room they look like worksheets.
- **Fix:** Build 6 to 8 genuine display posters with large text (40 pt or more), one idea each, and a strong visual:
  - Narrative structure, as an arc graphic
  - Somebody Wanted But So Then
  - Three types of adjectives
  - Adverbial phrases (when, where, how, why), as an icon grid
  - Six resolution types
  - Tension tools
  - Interaction protocols
- **Rename the rest:** Rename the current pages "Lesson word-bank cards".
- **Why it matters:** Buyers value matching classroom display, and cover 5 promises "Posters".

**8. Improve readability on the dense slides.**
- **Ordering slides:** On 101, 157, 173 and 352, the cards are at 14 to 15 pt with long sentences. Use no more than 6 cards, at 20 pt or more, in two columns.
- **Drag-card slides:** On 54, 197 and 386, the cards are 12 pt. Raise them to 16 pt or more, with fewer cards or wider ones.
- **Front matter:** On "Words we will use" (slides 4 to 6), the definitions are 15 pt at 6 per slide. Split them into 3 per slide, or move them to the teacher section.
- **Arcs:** The arcs on 247, 266 and 315 have floating boxes linked by tiny dashed stubs, with no continuous line. Draw one bold rising and falling line and sit the boxes on it.

**9. Add a one-page "week at a glance" program table with resources.**
- **Problem:** OTH's program table (Week, Lesson, Lesson sequence, Resources and notes) is one of the most visible things buyers value. Our unit of work has the content, but it is a 51-page document.
- **Fix:**
  - Add a 5-page "Week at a glance" (one page per week, landscape) to the front of the unit of work and to the preview, listing the slides, worksheet page numbers and the levels of each lesson.
  - Fix the near-empty overflow pages in the unit of work PDF (pp5, 8, 11 and 14 carry only 1 to 3 lines), so a buyer flicking through the preview does not see blank pages.

**10. Add co-construction and annotation slides, and say so in the listing.**
- **What LG does well:** It labels its blank "write it together" slides (pencil icon) and its assessment moments (purple box), and teachers clearly value this.
- **What we already have:** Plenty of shared-writing moments (for example, "Add the class's idea here" rows and "Type here" cells), but they are not signposted.
- **Fix:**
  - Add a small consistent badge on co-construction slides ("Write it together") and on assessment slides ("Assessment opportunity"), linked to the rubric.
  - Add a legend on "How this pack works" (slide 3).
  - Add a divider slide before the blank templates. At present slides 400 to 406 ("Type your title here") follow straight after the L20 exit ticket and look like leftover placeholders.

## 3. Where our pack looks worse than competitors

- **Sameness.** Within a few slides, the 18-slide-per-lesson rhythm and the single card template make the deck feel machine-made. BHB and LG look hand-made by a teacher, even where they are weaker. Our contact sheets for L1, L5, L13 and L19 are nearly indistinguishable.
- **Nothing on screen says "Mr Fox".** Competitors show the book cover and illustrations. That is legally risky for them, but it is instantly recognisable. Our Fox presence is a few fox photos and a fox emoji.
- **Empty book frames.** 23 projected slides have a large dashed empty box (for example slides 12, 34, 112 and 249). They read as "unfinished" rather than "copyright-safe".
- **Generic or mismatched emoji.** Examples:
  - a pear on "complication" (90)
  - a hammer or bone on "A good resolution" (366)
  - a lion and a mouse on the dilemmas slide (212)
  - a chicken on characterisation (32)
  - random 3D emoji (pumpkin, pear) on grammar slides

  These are decorative, not instructional.
- **Stock-photo children on Discuss slides.** The same few cut-out children recur on every Discuss and Connect slide (26, 44, 64, 84, 165, 203, 221, 261, 302, 342, 360, 380, 398). They are fine once, but repetitive across 20 lessons.
- **Small text in places** (see improvement 8). The worksheets are also visually dense at A4 (pp7 to 9, 13 to 15 and 18 to 20 have many numbered items in small type), while OTH's worksheets are simple big-box pages that Year 3 students find easier.
- **Duplicated speaker notes on about 140 slides** (see improvement 6).
- **The preview undersells the product.** The text is truncated, and it shows the plainest slides.
- **"Posters" are not posters** (see improvement 7).
- **Unit of work PDF orphan pages** (pp5, 8, 11 and 14).
- **Arc diagrams have broken connectors** (slides 247, 266 and 315; the worksheet arc on p31 has the same problem).

### Where we clearly win, and should say so loudly in the listing

- **Component A.** We have 20 full skill lessons. LG only marks Component A concepts, and the others have nothing.
- **Three-level differentiation** on every lesson and worksheet, with an answer key.
- **Assessment.** A 17-question test, an A to E rubric, a pre-test and 20 exit tickets.
- **Original model writing in the NSW handwriting font**, in 13 lessons.
- **Interactive drag sorts and hinge questions.**
- **Seven colour themes.**
- **Editable PPTX with notes and navigation.**
- **Copyright-safe.** Competitors embed the book cover, illustrations and full passages, and one links a PDF of the novel. That is a takedown risk for them, and a reassurance point for school-purchased licences.

---

## Verdict

On what is actually in the box, our pack is the strongest product in this niche by a wide margin. It is the only one with full Component A lessons, three-level differentiation throughout, real assessment with a rubric, original model texts and genuinely interactive slides. A teacher who bought it would get far more than from BHB, LG or OTH.

But TpT sales are won on the thumbnail and the first three preview images, and there we currently lose. Our cover does not say "Unit 1" or "Fantastic Mr Fox". The preview shows our plainest slides, with truncated text. The deck's single template, emoji clip art and empty book frames make it look more generic than the busier, hand-made-looking competitors.

Fix the cover and preview (improvements 1 and 2), restyle the book-passage slides and add Fox-relevant photos (3 and 4), de-duplicate the notes (6), and cut the repeated feedback and check slides (5). With those changes, the pack should clearly beat the best competitor on both first impression and substance. Without them, it risks being skipped in search results by buyers who never open the preview.
