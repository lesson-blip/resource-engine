# Theme research (20 Oct 2026)

Buyers are mostly women: about 72% of Australian education professionals are women (ABS Labour Force, Aug 2024, via APH FlagPost), and the share is higher in primary than secondary (AITSL ATWD 2023).

## What teacher stores are pushing for 2026
- Pastels lead: Spotty Pastel, Simply Pastel, Daisy Gingham, Pastel Composition (Miss Jacobs Little Learners 2026 trend and top picks).
- Calm and low clutter: soft greens, blues and neutrals; "less is more" walls (Miss Jacobs; Differentiation Corner, Mar 2026).
- Nature and botanical: earthy browns and greens, Modern Botanical, Woodland, Gum Leaves (Teach Starter AU; Miss Jacobs).
- Boho: Boho Rainbow and Boho Neutral are still listed as popular (Teach Starter AU blog and theme packs).
- Australiana: native flora and fauna, gum leaves, Australian animals (Teach Starter AU; Miss Jacobs "Australiana").
- Fading: cluttered walls, loud brights, chalkboard and 90s looks.

## Evidence on decoration
Fisher, Godwin & Seltman (2014): heavily decorated rooms reduced attention and learning in young children. Keep slides and worksheets calm: decoration at the edges, never behind content.

## Engine decision
Four themes, one engine: Ocean (default), Soft Pastel, Gum Leaf, Boho Neutral. Decoration sits only in clear corners and never overlaps content. Maths diagram colours stay high contrast in every theme.
